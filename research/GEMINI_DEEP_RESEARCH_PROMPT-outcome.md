SkyStacker State-of-the-Art Research Survey and Feasibility AssessmentThis report surveys 2024–2026 astrophotography and Go toolchain advances beyond SkyStacker's existing knowledge base and evaluates each technique against the project's single-file (main.go), CGO_ENABLED=0, standard-library-only float32 RGB architecture. The highest-impact, zero-dependency opportunities are upgrading the build toolchain from Go 1.20.3 to Go 1.26 (unlocking Swiss Table maps, the default Go 1.26 garbage collector, and faster binary I/O), adding finite-sample MAD scale corrections and single-pass sigma-clip accumulation, implementing scale=1.0 square-kernel CFA/Bayer drizzle on raw Bayer planes, and hardening the Gaia TAP client for the scheduled December 2, 2026 Gaia DR4 release. Beyond core stdlib processing, the June 2026 release of standalone cross-platform CLIs for RC-Astro tools alongside GraXpert v3.1+ CLIs enables optional external AI sidecar orchestration without embedding neural models, while embedded ONNX inference and amd64-only experimental simd/archsimd remain incompatible with SkyStacker's portability constraints.Executive SummaryGo 1.24–1.26 Stdlib and Runtime Gains [new] — Upgrading from SkyStacker's Go 1.20.3 baseline to Go 1.26 captures Go 1.24's Swiss Table maps (21.40% faster lookups and 31.65% faster inserts in a Go 1.24 RC2 microbenchmark blog, plus substantial heap savings on large maps) alongside Go 1.26's default garbage collector (7–11% overall GC throughput gain, 18–20% faster small-object GC, 15–20% faster encoding/binary, and ~2x faster large io.ReadAll in automated goperf.dev tracking) with zero dependency changes.RC-Astro Standalone Cross-Platform CLI (June 2026) [new] — Authoritative Siril and AstroPixelProcessor announcements confirm RC-Astro released standalone Windows, macOS, and Linux CLIs for BlurXTerminator, NoiseXTerminator, and StarXTerminator, eliminating PixInsight/Photoshop host lock-in. SkyStacker can orchestrate these licensed tools via temporary 32-bit float FITS sidecars without adding ML runtimes.GraXpert v3.0/v3.1+ and Cosmic Clarity Headless CLIs [adjacent] — Open-source GraXpert expanded its -cli interface beyond Background Extraction (-cmd background-extraction) to linear Denoising (-cmd denoising) and Object/Stellar Deconvolution (-cmd deconv-obj) backed by ONNX Runtime 1.20 and a -gpu false CPU mode. Seti Astro's Cosmic Clarity suite similarly matured into GPLv3 Seti Astro Suite Pro with walking-noise and linear restoration models.PixInsight 1.9.6 Finite-Sample Robust Scale Estimators [new] — PixInsight 1.9.6 Lockhart release notes document exact consistency constants and new finite-sample corrections across all robust scale estimators platform-wide. Applying finite-sample bias corrections to SkyStacker's MAD sigma-clipping for small sessions (N = 3–30) directly reduces over-rejection of genuine signal peaks and faint nebulosity tails.Single-Pass Sigma-Clipping and FITS Rice Compression in ASTAP (July 2026) [adjacent] — ASTAP's authoritative changelog reports a 25% speedup in sigma-clip stacking by computing mean and variance in one pass, alongside native FITS Rice tile compression (.fits.fz) while omitting GZIP FITS. Single-pass two-moment accumulation fits SkyStacker's tiled spill-file reducers cleanly.XISF 1.0 Specification Revision 2 and Strict Conformance [adjacent] — PixInsight 1.9.6 conforms to XISF Specification Revision 2 with parallel compressed I/O (1.5–4.6x faster writes, 1.8–4x faster reads, 3.3–4x faster checksummed I/O) and SHA3-256/SHA3-512 block checksums. Adjacent open-source XISF tooling (openastro-ara) reinforces strict sample-mapping verification and rejection of corrupt data blocks.CFA/Bayer Drizzle Nuances for Modern Small-Pixel CMOS [adjacent] — Official Siril documentation establishes that CFA drizzle with a mathematically flux-preserving square kernel at scale=1.0 eliminates demosaicing color artifacts without inflating scratch-space requirements like 2x upscaling, whereas 2x CFA upscaling produces 4x-equivalent coverage sparsity and impairs outlier rejection. Practitioner reports recommend random non-integer dithering and >=15–30 subframes.Gaia DR4 Archive v3.15 Quotas and Blackout Schedule [KB-covered] — Authoritative ESA bulletins confirm Gaia DR4 (66 months of data) is scheduled for 2 December 2026 at 12:00 CET following a 25 November–2 December 2026 archive blackout, under Archive v3.15 rules capping users at 5 parallel jobs, a 50-job queue, 50,000 rows, and tightened timeouts with renamed DataLink identifiers (DATAMODEL_GAIA, DATAMODEL_STANDARD).Lightweight Open-Source Plate-Solving and Gradient Detrending [new] — Alongside ASTAP's 2025–2026 improvements for star-poor images, Cedar Solve (an open-source evolution of Tetra3) and Cedar Detect offer modern permissive star detection and blind solving, while ASTAP's maintainer demonstrated that target-aware gradient removal rescues failed plate solves on gradient-heavy frames.Infeasible Embedded ML and Experimental SIMD Lock-In [new] — Polaris Astro documentation places GraXpert's ONNX denoise weights alone at 284 MB (v2) to 456 MB (v3), confirming embedded inference is incompatible with a small CGO_ENABLED=0 static binary; likewise, Go 1.26's simd/archsimd remains an off-by-default amd64-only GOEXPERIMENT unsuitable for cross-platform distribution.Domain-by-Domain FindingsA. Stacking Statistics and OutliersSmall-N Pixel Rejection and Weighted IntegrationStatus: (a) already in the KB for Winsorized, percentile, and GESDT concepts; (c) genuinely new to this project for PixInsight 1.9.6 finite-sample scale corrections and ASTAP single-pass sigma-clipping.Authoritative PixInsight 1.9.6 Lockhart release notes (date uncertain) introduce exact consistency constants and finite-sample corrections for all robust scale estimators platform-wide. Standard asymptotic factors (such as 1.4826 for MAD-to-Gaussian-sigma) underestimate scale at small sample sizes (N = 3–30), causing standard MAD sigma-clipping to over-reject legitimate signal variation in small amateur sessions. Complementing accuracy, ASTAP's July 2026 changelog reports a 25% stacking speedup in sigma-clip mode by combining mean and variance measurement into one pass (following an earlier ~25% threading speedup in Feb 2025). In frame weighting, Seti Astro Suite Pro (2025–2026) applies weighted Blink quality scores so suboptimal frames are downweighted rather than discarded.No 2024–2026 comparative benchmarks evaluating MAD variants, Winsorized sigma, linear-fit clipping, and GESDT specifically on faint extended nebulosity, nor new float32 variance-propagation studies, were found in the gathered material. For SkyStacker—whose measured OrionNebula benchmarks involve 4 to 16 frames—adding finite-sample MAD correction tables and fusing mean/variance estimation into single-pass tiled accumulation directly improves both small-N statistical fidelity and CPU throughput.Drizzle and CFA/Bayer DrizzleStatus: (b) adjacent but not implemented (currently an explicit non-goal in SkyStacker, with CFA drizzle unassessed in the KB).Authoritative Siril documentation (2025–2026) and AstroPixelProcessor (APP) 2.0.0-beta47 discussions establish clear technical constraints for CFA/Bayer drizzle. Siril's Square drizzle kernel distributes each input pixel's flux in exact proportion to overlap area, guaranteeing mathematical flux conservation essential for diffuse nebulosity, whereas Point kernels lower correlated noise at high risk of null-pixel holes. Crucially, because a Bayer CFA records green at 50% density and red/blue at 25% density, CFA drizzle at scale=1.0 already incurs the droplet-coverage sparsity of a 2x mono drizzle; upscaling on top requires 4x-equivalent coverage. Siril also notes that null pixels from small droplets reduce effective sample counts available for outlier rejection.Practitioner reports on Cloudy Nights and PixInsight tutorials agree that CFA drizzle at scale=1.0 eliminates demosaicing color artifacts, suppresses green midtone bias, and produces finer-grained noise even on well-sampled small-pixel CMOS sensors, provided frames have random non-integer dithers (e.g., 2.45 or 3.65 px). Practitioners report good results with 15–30 frames, acceptable results at 10–15 frames, and pattern/hole artifacts below 10 frames, while noting that 1.5x–2x drizzle can consume massive scratch disk space (one practitioner reported 1.25 TB in APP for 8 hours of M51 at 1.5x/1.5, compared to minimal overhead at scale=1.0 and droplet 0.7).For SkyStacker, implementing scale=1.0 square-kernel CFA drizzle on raw Bayer planes (dcraw -D -4) directly improves OSC color fidelity and noise texture while staying within the developer host's 15 GiB RAM envelope.D. ## Implementation status: single-pass sigma-clip (2026-11-17, append-only)

Proposal 2 ("single-pass mean + variance sigma-clip") was implemented in a conservative form: mean accumulation was folded into the last clipping pass, the survivor list is carried between iterations, and tile sums are gathered in one pass. Median/MAD rejection was deliberately retained, because replacing it with mean/variance would change rejection semantics and break bit-exact equivalence. `testSinglePassSigmaClipEquivalence` verifies exact float32 outputs against the original two-pass implementations (now kept as test-only references), and an OrionNebula 4-frame sigma stack produced a byte-identical `stacked_mean.f32raw`. Microbenchmarks show ~20–25% faster kernels; end-to-end gains are masked by decoding and I/O. FITS Rice and XISF Rev 2 items from this survey remain open.
