# SkyStacker Research

This folder contains background research, technical notes, and design explorations that inform the development of SkyStacker.

**Start here:** [RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md](RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md) — a consolidated, up-to-date synthesis of the current implementation status, mathematical foundations, architecture principles, known limitations, and prioritized roadmap.

The other files are source material (analyses, detailed specifications, and reference catalogs) that were used to produce the synthesis. They remain available for deeper reading but may contain overlapping or partially outdated content.
